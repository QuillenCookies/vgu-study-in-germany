Create the Community Page for a "Study in Germany" platform.

**# PURPOSE**
Provide information about universities, public transportation, housing, food, and entertainment for international students in Germany.

**# TECH STACK**

* Backend: Django + SQLite (Focus only on the frontend for this task)
* Frontend: React JS, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
* Architecture: Component-based design

# Frontend Specification: The Duck Pond (Community & Forum)

## 1. Page Narrative
- **Core Concept:** "You don't have to swim the Rhine alone."
- **The Data Science Angle:** Transforming the chaotic "scattered data" of student life into a structured "Knowledge Base."
- **Tone:** Collaborative, expert-led (Alumni), and supportive.

---

## 2. Hero Section: "Join the Flock"
- **Headline:** From VGU to Germany: A Shared Journey.
- **Sub-headline:** "Individually, we are just scattered data points. Together, we are a map. Connect with VGU Alumni, share your 'Notes,' and help us build the ultimate survival guide."
- **Visual:** A network graph visualization (Node-link diagram) where the nodes are little duck icons, representing the connection between VGU students and Alumni.

---

## 3. Section: "Footprints from the Flock" (The Alumni Notes)
*A curated feed of deep-dive insights from seniors who have already "migrated."*

- **Card Title:** [The City Name] - Survival Log
- **Snippet:** "The most important thing I learned about my first winter in Munich wasn't on a brochure..."
- **Metadata:** Author: VGU Alumni '22 | Topic: Life Hacks | Insight Score: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (A nod to your Data Science interest).
- **CTA:** "Read Full Note"

## 4. Section: "Quack-Talk" (The Forum)
*A high-frequency, categorized forum layout for quick questions.*

- **Categories (Bento Grid Style):**
    1. **The Bureaucracy Maze:** (Visa, Anmeldung, Health Insurance) - "Deciphering the paperwork."
    2. **Academic Nest:** (Exams, Thesis, Prof. tips) - "Passing the German way."
    3. **The Marketplace:** (Furniture, Books, WG spots) - "One duck's trash, another duck's treasure."
