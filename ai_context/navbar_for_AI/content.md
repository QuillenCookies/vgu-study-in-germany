# Website Navigation Structure: Study in Germany Guide

## 1. Brand Identity
- **Name:** Study in Germany Guide
- **Tagline:** Practical tips for international students.

---

## 2. Navigation Menu Hierarchy

### [1] Home
- **Label:** Home
- **Destination:** /home

### [2] Explore (Main Category Hub)
- **Label:** Explore
- **Structure:** Mega Menu / Multi-column Dropdown
- **Sub-Categories:**
    1. **Education**
       - Label: Education
       - Content: University, Enrollment, Exams.
    2. **Transportation**
       - Label: Transportation
       - Content: DB Bahn, Semester Ticket, Local transport.
    3. **Housing**
       - Label: Housing
       - Content: Student dorms, WG (Flatshare), Anmeldung.
    4. **Entertainment**
       - Label: Entertainment
       - Content: Nightlife, Festivals, Sightseeing.
    5. **Food**
       - Label: Food
       - Content: Mensa, Grocery shopping, German cuisine.

### [3] About
- **Label:** About
- **Destination:** /about

### [4] Tools
- **Label:** Tools
- **Sub-Items:**
    - **Living budget calculation**
      - Label: Living budget calculation
      - Destination: /tools/budget-calculator

### [5] Community
- **Label:** Community
- **Sub-Items:**
    - **Contributor**
      - Label: Contributor
      - Destination: /community/contributor
